# devNepal privacy notice

Effective date: September 2026

This notice explains how devNepal handles information about members, GitHub contributors, and visitors. We index public contributions to participating GitHub repositories whether or not the contributor has a devNepal account. Indexing a contribution does not create a devNepal account or member profile.

## Who is responsible for your information

SDOC oversees how devNepal handles personal information, including privacy requests and access to confidential data. You can reach its designated team through the private contact channel specified by SDOC.

## GitHub contribution records

We use public GitHub information to maintain a record of contributions to participating repositories. This includes contribution activity and the GitHub identifiers and usernames needed to attribute the work. These records are separate from devNepal accounts and member profiles.

If you choose to sign in with or connect a GitHub account you control, devNepal can associate that account's previously indexed contributions with your devNepal account and show you that history. We verify your control of the GitHub account before linking it. You do not need a devNepal account to contribute on GitHub.

## Accounts and sign-in

You can create a devNepal account using email or supported sign-in services, including GitHub, Google, Facebook, and other recognised providers offered on the sign-in page. A GitHub connection is required to participate as a developer or repository contributor through devNepal. You can use other sign-in methods to comment and give feedback on devNepal without connecting GitHub. Only authorised government bodies can create projects on devNepal.

We store the information needed to create and manage your account. When you use an external sign-in service, this may include an account identifier, name, profile picture, and email address, depending on the service and the permissions you grant. With email sign-in, we use the email address you provide. We use your account email for account-related communication.

Your initial profile picture may come from your chosen sign-in service, including GitHub when you use it.

## Information you add

Your profile can include a display name, a short headline, an organisation or institution you are associated with, a location, a biography, and links. We store the details you enter so that you can introduce yourself in the member directory. Most profile details are optional.

We also store comments and feedback you submit through devNepal so that you can take part in discussions and share suggestions. For projects created by authorised government bodies, we store the project information submitted on their behalf.

If you apply to help lead a project's founding team, we collect the experience and supporting information you submit so that SDOC can assess your application. Applications are not public and are reviewed only by people authorised by SDOC.

## What other people can see

A new profile is not publicly visible until SDOC approves it. Once approved, anyone can view the public version, including people who are not signed in.

Profile approval governs publication in the member directory. GitHub contribution records are indexed separately. Comments, feedback, and project information you submit to public pages are visible to anyone who can access those pages.

Contact details used for account communication or recovery are private by default. Publishing your profile does not automatically make them public. You choose which contact details or communication links, if any, to display on your public profile.

This choice applies to public contact details. Account-recovery settings, verification codes, and security credentials remain private.

If you write contact details in a biography or another field that you choose to publish, those details will be visible to anyone who can view that field.

Your connection to an employer, university, or other organisation is self-declared and is not verified by the government.

Only include information in your public profile that you are comfortable sharing. Other people may copy public information, and search engines may index it. Removing a profile from devNepal cannot guarantee removal of copies held elsewhere.

## Cookies and technical information

devNepal uses essential cookies to keep you signed in and protect sign-in and form submissions from misuse. Cookies are small pieces of information stored by your browser. You can block or delete them through your browser settings, but signing in and some other functions may then stop working.

We collect limited technical information to operate the service, identify faults, and protect against abuse. This may include your IP address, browser and device information, request times, error details, and sign-in or security events. We also keep audit records of administrative actions to help protect personal information and investigate unauthorised access or changes.

We limit these records to information needed for those purposes and exclude passwords, sign-in codes, and authentication secrets from logs. Technical and audit records are not public. Access is limited to people authorised by SDOC, and retention follows the policy defined by SDOC.

External sign-in services may use their own cookies when you use them. Their privacy notices, linked below, explain how those cookies and related information are handled.

## Who can access your information

Private account information is not available to other members through the directory, contribution records, or public posts.

Access to non-public information is limited to people specifically authorised by SDOC, and only to the information needed for their assigned responsibilities. Being a contributor or repository maintainer does not itself grant access to confidential information.

## Hosting and external services

All devNepal hosting is provided exclusively within Government of Nepal facilities. This includes the application, databases, file storage, and backups.

GitHub hosts source code, repository issues, pull requests, and reviews. devNepal supports member comments and feedback, with project creation limited to authorised government bodies. External sign-in services operate separately from devNepal's government-hosted infrastructure.

Your use of an external service is covered by its own privacy notice, including [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement), [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), and [Meta's privacy policy for Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/). Other supported sign-in services and websites linked from devNepal have their own privacy notices.

## Changing or deleting your information

After signing in, you can edit your own devNepal profile. Changes to certain fields require SDOC's approval before they appear publicly. While those changes are under review, your last approved profile remains public. The updated version replaces it once SDOC approves the changes.

You can also delete your profile yourself. SDOC's retention policy governs how account information, indexed GitHub contributions, and your comments, feedback, and project submissions are handled after profile deletion. Indexing later GitHub activity does not create or restore a devNepal account or profile.

For help with your account, profile, or incorrectly linked contributions, contact SDOC's designated team through the private contact channel specified by SDOC. You can also use this route to request correction or removal of indexed information even if you do not have a devNepal account. Include your devNepal account identifier or the relevant GitHub username and a brief description of the request. The team may ask you to verify control of the relevant account before making changes.

Use this private contact route rather than a public GitHub issue for requests involving personal information. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, or identity documents with your initial request.

Removing a devNepal profile or account does not delete your accounts with external sign-in services or remove issues, pull requests, or other contributions you have made on GitHub.

## How long we keep information

We retain personal information in accordance with the retention policy defined by SDOC. This policy governs account and profile information, founding-team applications, indexed GitHub contribution records, comments, feedback, and project submissions, including information associated with inactive or deleted accounts and profiles.

## Changes to this notice

SDOC oversees revisions to this notice. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

Significant changes to how your personal information is collected, used, shared, or retained will be announced on devNepal before they take effect. The announcement will explain what is changing and when.
